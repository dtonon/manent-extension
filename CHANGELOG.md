# Changelog

Notable changes to the Manent extension. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com); entries under Unreleased become the next release's notes.

## [Unreleased]

The first release: save what you find on the web as Manent notes, signed with your Nostr key and published to your relays, from Chrome and Firefox.

- Log in with an nsec or an ncryptsec (NIP-49), or sign with a NIP-07 extension
- Save the page link, the full article in readable form, or the selected text, with a comment and tags, from the popup or the page context menu
- Save images with a comment, uploaded to your Blossom servers, and mark them as sensitive
- Save a screenshot of an area you drag on the page, or of the whole view; pages without a readable article are saved as a screenshot
- Save YouTube videos and Shorts, Instagram reels, photo posts and carousels, and Facebook reels and photos with caption, author, date and cover, tagged with #video and the site
- Open the popup with Alt+Shift+M, or save the page link without opening it with Alt+Shift+S
- Retry saves that failed to reach the relays from the popup, or automatically when the extension starts
- See the relays and Blossom servers in use from the avatar menu, with your profile picture and name
- Get a confirmation card on the page after each save
